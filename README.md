# 🌾 Kisan Mitra – Smart Agriculture Decision Agent

> **AI-powered agricultural decision-support platform for farmers**

Kisan Mitra is a smart agriculture platform designed to provide farmers with essential agricultural information, AI-powered crop disease detection, farming assistance, weather advisories, market prices, government schemes, and verified farmer support services through a single easy-to-use application.

The platform aims to reduce the difficulty farmers face in accessing reliable agricultural information and provide practical assistance in their day-to-day farming decisions.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers often need information from multiple sources for different agricultural decisions.

For example:

- Identifying crop diseases requires expert knowledge.
- Weather conditions can affect irrigation and spraying.
- Market prices change frequently.
- Government schemes and subsidies can be difficult to discover.
- Farmers may not know which agricultural officer to contact.
- Language barriers can make digital agricultural services difficult to use.
- Getting quick answers to farming questions may not always be possible.

Kisan Mitra brings these services together into a single platform.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

Kisan Mitra provides an integrated agricultural assistant with:

🌱 **AI Crop Disease Detection**

🤖 **Kisan AI Copilot**

🌦️ **Weather Advisories**

💰 **Mandi Market Prices**

🏛️ **Government Schemes**

📞 **Farmer Support & Officer Contacts**

📅 **Village Farming Calendar**

🌐 **Native Language Support**

The objective is to provide farmers with information and assistance in a simple and accessible manner.

# 🚀 Key Features

## 🌱 1. Crop Disease AI Lens

Farmers can upload an image of a crop or affected plant.

The AI analyzes the image and predicts the possible crop disease.
